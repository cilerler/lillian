## Role Summary

A short paragraph describing what the person does in general — the kind of work, the systems they touch, and the impact. Keep it natural, not prescriptive. Day-to-day responsibilities shift; this paragraph captures the *shape* of the role, not a deliverables list.

> *Example:* We're looking for a passionate Senior Full-Stack .NET Developer to help us maintain and improve our code base and systems. The person works across the .NET stack — backend services, data, frontend, and operational tooling — collaborating with the team on whatever the highest-leverage problem of the week happens to be.
